A LITTLE NOTE FOR YOU

Honestly, I don't even know where to begin, but I have had this appreciation in my heart for quite some time, and I think it's finally time I put it into words.

We have known each other since 2020, back when we were still in high school. From 2020 to 2023, we shared a chapter of life that I will always look back on with a smile.

I want you to know that I genuinely appreciate you. Not just for what you did, but for who you were to me. You were amazing both in and out of school, and I don't think I ever properly expressed how much I valued that.

Thank you for your support, your kindness, your understanding, your time and the way you were always good to me. Thank you for the conversations, the laughter, the advice and all those little moments that made our friendship special.

Sometimes we get so comfortable having certain people around that we forget to remind them how much they mean to us. I think that's something I failed to do. I never really got the chance to sit down and tell you how grateful I was to have you in my life.

Now that we are far away from each other, I find it even more important to say these things. Life has taken us in different directions, and we might not share the same everyday moments we once did, but that doesn't mean I have forgotten them.

I still appreciate the time we spent together. I appreciate the person you were to me, the memories we created and the friendship we shared.

I genuinely hope life is treating you well. I hope you are happy, achieving your goals, growing and becoming everything you have ever wanted to be.

I don't know if I have ever said this properly, but THANK YOU. Thank you for being part of my life and for giving me memories I will always appreciate.

This letter might have come later than it should have, but my appreciation for you is genuine.

No matter how far life takes us, I will always be grateful that I got to know you.

With sincere appreciation,

MR Z.Zwelonke ♡
